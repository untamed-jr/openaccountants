# California eWaste fee on video display devices, 2026 and 2027 v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from Public Resources Code (PRC) 42463, 42464, 42464.2, 42464.4, 42464.6, 42475.2 and 42485 (current text read in the official California Legislature bulk data archive, with the CDTFA Business Taxes Law Guide copy); CDTFA's Covered Electronic Waste Recycling Fees Guide (Getting Started and Industry Topics pages), its Electronic Waste Recycling Fee Return filing instructions, special notice L-1023 (January 2026), Publication 95 (11-23) and its special taxes and fees rate table; CalRecycle's rulemaking page and documents for the 2025 video display device fee emergency regulation (14 CCR 18660.40), its program and fee pages, and its covered battery-embedded products Q&A (15 May 2026). All were read on 2026-11-23 (California, Pacific time). No accountant has reviewed it.

**Answer first.** A consumer who buys or leases a new or refurbished video display device (VDD) with a screen over four inches that DTSC's regulations identify pays the eWaste fee; the retailer collects it at the sale, shows it separately on the receipt, may keep 3%, and pays the rest to CDTFA with an electronic return (PRC 42464(a), (c), (d), (f); 42464.4). For 2026 and 2027 the fee per device is:

| Screen size, measured diagonally | Fee from 1 January 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| More than 4 inches, less than 15 inches | USD 4 |
| 15 inches or more, less than 35 inches | USD 5 |
| 35 inches or more | USD 6 |

CalRecycle "made the determination to maintain the Fee at the current rates in each screen size category", and its emergency regulation amending 14 CCR 18660.40 was approved by the Office of Administrative Law on 29 September 2025, effective 1 January 2026, and "will expire on January 1, 2028" ([CalRecycle rulemaking page](https://calrecycle.ca.gov/laws/rulemaking/rfvdd/); [CalRecycle emergency rulemaking action](https://www2.calrecycle.ca.gov/Docs/Web/131793)). CDTFA's January 2026 notice confirms the same three rates ([CDTFA L-1023](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/L1023.pdf)). The statute still prints USD 6, 8 and 10 (PRC 42464(a)); those are the base amounts CalRecycle reviews and adjusts under PRC 42464(g), and the regulation's amounts apply.

**What is covered** (CDTFA's list, from DTSC's regulations, [CDTFA Industry Topics](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/covered-electronic-waste-recycling-fee/industry-topics.htm)): televisions (CRT, LCD, LED, plasma, OLED; not LCD or plasma projection TVs), desktop computer monitors, laptops, tablets, bare CRTs, portable DVD players with LCD or LED screens, and LCD or LED smart displays. Not covered: cell phones and smartphones, eReaders, displays in motor vehicles, in industrial, commercial or medical equipment, or in listed household appliances (PRC 42463(g)(2); [CDTFA eWaste return](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/online-filing-instructions/ewaste-fee-return.htm)). A laptop or tablet with a built-in battery pays this fee, not the battery-embedded fee: "A VDD is not a CBEP" ([CalRecycle Q&A](https://www2.calrecycle.ca.gov/Docs/Web/137908); PRC 42463(f)(2)(B)).

**Tax year.** Tax year label 2026-27: the rates above apply to sales in 2026 and 2027. CalRecycle's 2027 battery-embedded fee regulation, approved by OAL on 24 September 2026 and effective 1 January 2027, amends the same section 18660.40 and restates subdivisions (a) to (c) unchanged: "On or after January 1, 2026, four dollars ($4)", five and six dollars ([OAL-approved 2027 regulation text](https://www2.calrecycle.ca.gov/Docs/Web/140116); [CalRecycle 2027 battery-fee rulemaking page](https://calrecycle.ca.gov/laws/rulemaking/cbewrfer/)). CDTFA's November 2026 notice L-1047 changes only the battery-embedded fee cap. None of the 91 waste, recycling, electronics, budget, resources, environment, battery and fee bills chaptered in 2026 in the Legislature's archive amends PRC 42463 or 42464. No change to the video display device fee for 2027 was found as of the read date.

**Circular 230 disclosure.** This material is general information, not written advice on a specific transaction under 31 CFR 10.37. It is not intended to be used, and cannot be used, to avoid penalties.

## Scope and who this is for

In scope: retailers (including manufacturers selling direct and marketplace facilitators), lessors, vendors that collect the fee for lessors, out-of-state sellers registered for California sales or use tax, and consumers who bought without paying the fee; and their bookkeepers. The job: decide which devices carry the fee, compute it by screen band, and register, file and pay.

Out of scope, and where it goes:

| Topic | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Covered battery-embedded waste recycling fee on smartphones, e-bikes, power tools and other battery-embedded products | the covered battery-embedded waste fee skill (separate return); not covered here |
| Whether a particular device is a covered VDD | DTSC (CDTFA cannot decide it, PRC 42464.6); not covered beyond CDTFA's list |
| Manufacturer notices, reports and recycling payments | CalRecycle; refer |
| Cell phone take-back program | refer |
| Sales and use tax on the device | california-sales-tax |

## Ask the client first

- Is the business a California retailer, a lessor, a vendor to lessors, a marketplace facilitator, or an out-of-state seller? Does it hold an eWaste fee account, and what filing period did CDTFA assign?
- For each device sold or leased in the period: is it new or refurbished, what kind of device, and what is its viewable screen size?
- Which sales were for resale, shipped directly outside California, or to Native Americans on a reservation? Which devices were returned, and when was each originally sold?
- Did the business take devices out of inventory for its own use or to donate?
- For leases: does the lessor collect the fee from lessees, or pay it to a registered vendor and say so on the lease documents?
- Did the business absorb the fee for customers, and does the receipt say so?

### Steps

1. **Is it a covered VDD?** A covered electronic device includes "a video display device containing a screen greater than four inches, measured diagonally, that is identified in the regulations adopted by DTSC" (PRC 42463(g)(1)(A)). Use CDTFA's list (televisions, monitors, laptops, tablets, bare CRTs, portable DVD players, smart displays). Excluded by statute: displays in a motor vehicle; displays "contained within, or a part of a piece of industrial, commercial, or medical equipment, including monitoring or control equipment"; displays in listed appliances (PRC 42463(g)(2)). CDTFA also lists as exempt "A cell phone, including smartphone with screen size greater than four inches", "An eReader, eBook, or eInk" and LCD or plasma projection televisions ([CDTFA eWaste return](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/online-filing-instructions/ewaste-fee-return.htm)). New devices DTSC adds to its regulations become subject to the fee from 1 July of the following year (CDTFA Industry Topics).
2. **Is the sale subject to the fee?** The fee applies to the retail sale or lease of a new or refurbished device, by any channel, and to sales to the U.S. government ("Sales to the U.S. government are not exempt from the fee"). It does not apply to used devices that are not refurbished, to sales for resale, to sales "to American Indians on Indian reservations", or to devices shipped directly outside California "if the transaction is not subject to California sales or use tax"; "If the consumer takes possession of the product in California, the fee applies" (CDTFA eWaste return). Keep records supporting each excluded sale.
3. **Pick the band by viewable screen size** and multiply the number of devices in each band by its fee. Exactly 15 inches is in the USD 5 band; exactly 35 inches is in the USD 6 band (14 CCR 18660.40).
4. **Collect and show it.** "The retailer shall separately state" the fee on the receipt (PRC 42464(f)). A retailer may pay the fee for the customer instead; it must say so on the receipt, and then "the fee is a debt owed by the retailer to the state, and the consumer is not liable for the fee" (PRC 42464(e)(1)). Do not include the fee in the sales tax measure: "The eWaste fee and CBE waste recycling fee are not included in the measure of sales or use tax" (CDTFA Industry Topics).
5. **Leases.** For a lease that is a continuing sale, the lessor must collect the eWaste fee "from the lessee with the first lease payment"; lease renewals are not subject to the fee unless a new or refurbished device is provided (CDTFA Industry Topics). Instead, the lessor may pay the fee to its vendor if the vendor is registered with CDTFA for the fee, holds a seller's permit, separately states the fee on its invoice, and the lessor states on the lease documents that the fee has been paid; the fee is then the vendor's debt, and the vendor keeps the 3% (PRC 42464(d)(2), (e)(2), (e)(3)). Publication 95: lessors that make no sales, only lease, and meet those conditions "are not required to register and collect or pay the fee".
6. **Self-consumed stock and out-of-state sellers.** A retailer that removes a device from inventory to use or donate it is the consumer and reports the fee (CDTFA Industry Topics; the return's "self-consumed" line). An out-of-state seller required to hold a California seller's permit or use tax registration must register and collect; others may register voluntarily; otherwise "the California customer must pay the fee directly to us" (CDTFA Industry Topics).
7. **Compute the remittance.** Gross fee = sum over bands of devices x fee. The retailer "may retain 3 percent" and "shall transmit the remainder" (PRC 42464(d)(1)). Refunds on returned devices are credited on the return: returns of sales made in the same period on the current-period screen, earlier periods on Schedule A, which also carries a "3% fee collection reimbursement adjustment" (CDTFA eWaste return). There is no credit for bad debts (CDTFA Industry Topics). Fee collected in excess of the amount due is reported and paid (CDTFA eWaste return).
8. **File and pay.** The fee is "due and payable quarterly on or before the last day of the month following each calendar quarter", with a return "filed using electronic media", and CDTFA "may require" other periods (PRC 42464.4, current text). CDTFA's Getting Started page says the return is filed "on either a quarterly or yearly filing basis"; Publication 95 (11-23) says CDTFA assigns "a monthly, quarterly, or annual reporting period". Use the period on the account. All locations go on one return; a return is required even with no activity (CDTFA eWaste return). CDTFA: "If the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or state holiday, we will consider payments postmarked or received by the next business day to be on time" (CDTFA Getting Started).

**AUDIT FLASH POINT** (which devices and which band). Disputes over whether an item "is or is not a covered electronic device" are not decided by CDTFA: it "shall not accept or consider" a petition or refund claim founded on that ground and forwards it to DTSC (PRC 42464.6). Retailers that treat a tablet or laptop with a built-in battery as a battery-embedded product (1.5%) instead of a VDD (flat USD 4 or 5), band a 15-inch laptop at USD 4, or drop self-consumed display stock, are the usual audit points. Keep screen-size and product-type records for every device.

## Worked cases

Both cases are made up for illustration.

**Case 1: a retailer's October to December 2026 return.**

- **Facts:** an electronics retailer with an eWaste account on a quarterly period. New devices sold in the quarter:
  - 120 tablets and small laptops with screens of 10 to 14 inches.
  - 300 computer monitors with 27-inch screens, of which 20 were sold to another retailer on a resale certificate.
  - 40 laptops with screens of exactly 15.0 inches.
  - 80 televisions with 65-inch screens, of which 10 were shipped by the retailer to buyers in Arizona in transactions not subject to California sales or use tax.
  - 500 smartphones (not VDDs; no eWaste fee).
  - No returns, no self-consumed devices.
- **Count by band:** USD 4 band 120; USD 5 band 300 - 20 + 40 = 320; USD 6 band 80 - 10 = 70.
- **Gross fee:** 120 x 4 + 320 x 5 + 70 x 6 = 480 + 1,600 + 420 = **USD 2,500**.
- **3% reimbursement:** USD 75. **Remit:** USD 2,425, due 31 January 2027 (a Sunday; CDTFA treats a payment postmarked or received on Monday 1 February 2027 as on time).

```oa-check
name: eWaste fee quarterly return, October to December 2026
inputs:
  band1_sold: 120
  band2_monitors_sold: 300
  band2_resale: 20
  band2_laptops_15in: 40
  band3_sold: 80
  band3_shipped_out_of_state: 10
  fee_band1: 4
  fee_band2: 5
  fee_band3: 6
  retention_rate: 0.03
steps:
  - band1_units: band1_sold
  - band2_units: band2_monitors_sold - band2_resale + band2_laptops_15in
  - band3_units: band3_sold - band3_shipped_out_of_state
  - gross_fee: band1_units * fee_band1 + band2_units * fee_band2 + band3_units * fee_band3
  - retained: round(gross_fee * retention_rate, 2)
  - remit: gross_fee - retained
expected:
  band1_units: 120
  band2_units: 320
  band3_units: 70
  gross_fee: 2500
  retained: 75.00
  remit: 2425.00
```

**Case 2: a lessor deciding who pays the fee on 200 new monitors, January to March 2027.**

- **Facts:** an equipment lessor that makes no sales leases 200 new 24-inch monitors to California businesses under continuing-sale leases. It buys them from a vendor that is registered with CDTFA for the eWaste fee and holds a seller's permit. Fee per monitor: USD 5.
- **Route A, collect from lessees:** the lessor registers, collects USD 1,000 from the lessees with the first lease payments, keeps 3% (USD 30), files the return and remits USD 970 by 30 April 2027. The lessees fund the fee; the lessor's net cash on the fee is +USD 30.
- **Route B, pay the vendor:** the lessor pays the USD 1,000 fee to the vendor "on behalf of the consumer" (PRC 42464(e)(2)), separately stated on the vendor's invoice, and states on each lease that the fee has been paid. The vendor owes the fee, keeps the USD 30 and remits USD 970. Because the lessor makes no sales and meets the conditions, Publication 95 says it is "not required to register and collect or pay the fee". The lessor's net cash on the fee is -USD 1,000 unless it builds the cost into its rents.
- **What decides it:** the state receives USD 970 either way. Route A costs the lessor nothing and earns USD 30, but needs an eWaste account and a return every period. Route B removes the account and the returns but costs the lessor USD 1,000 out of pocket, which it must recover through rent. The decision turns on whether the lessor can price the fee into its leases and how much the extra account costs to run.

```oa-check
name: eWaste fee lessor decision, collect from lessees or pay the vendor
inputs:
  monitors: 200
  fee_per_monitor: 5
  retention_rate: 0.03
steps:
  - gross_fee: monitors * fee_per_monitor
  - retained_by_collector: round(gross_fee * retention_rate, 2)
  - state_receives: gross_fee - retained_by_collector
  - route_a_lessor_net_cash: gross_fee - (gross_fee - retained_by_collector)
  - route_b_lessor_net_cash: 0 - gross_fee
  - route_b_vendor_keeps: retained_by_collector
  - gap_between_routes: route_a_lessor_net_cash - route_b_lessor_net_cash
expected:
  gross_fee: 1000
  retained_by_collector: 30.00
  state_receives: 970.00
  route_a_lessor_net_cash: 30.00
  route_b_lessor_net_cash: -1000
  route_b_vendor_keeps: 30.00
  gap_between_routes: 1030.00
```

## When to refuse or refer

- **Device classification.** Whether a product is a covered VDD: DTSC (PRC 42464.6); battery-embedded products: CalRecycle and the battery-embedded fee skill.
- **Penalty relief, refunds and appeals.** Refer to CDTFA under the Fee Collection Procedures Law.
- **Manufacturer obligations and recycler payments.** CalRecycle.
- **2028 and later rates.** The 2025 VDD package expires on 1 January 2028; the 2027 battery package that restates the VDD amounts expires on 2 January 2029. Check CalRecycle and CDTFA for the fee from 2028.

## Filing and payment

- **Registration:** CDTFA online services, "Register a New Business Activity", for an eWaste fee account. Required for California retailers and lessors of VDDs (including marketplace facilitators), consumers who did not pay the fee at purchase, and vendors collecting under a lessor's election (CDTFA Getting Started).
- **Return:** Electronic Waste Recycling Fee Return, filed online for every period, even with no activity; all locations on one return. It asks for the number of VDDs sold or leased and self-consumed, items not subject to the fee (interstate or foreign commerce, resale, other), the number in each screen band, current-period returns and fee-paid purchases resold, prior-period credits on Schedule A, and excess fee collected (CDTFA eWaste return; L-1023).
- **Due:** last day of the month after the period. For quarterly filers: 30 April 2026, 31 July 2026, 31 October 2026 (Saturday), 31 January 2027 (Sunday), 30 April 2027, 31 July 2027 (Saturday), 31 October 2027 (Sunday), 31 January 2028.
- **Payment:** online; EFT if CDTFA requires it (CDTFA Getting Started).
- **Late:** "The penalty for filing a late return and/or late fee payment is ten percent of the fee amount due for the period", plus interest (CDTFA Getting Started; return instructions citing Fee Collection Procedures Law section 55042). Extensions and penalty relief are requested online.
- **Closing:** notify CDTFA and file the final return by the last day of the month after the quarter in which the business closes or is sold (Publication 95).

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Each device checked against CDTFA's VDD list and the statutory exclusions; cell phones and eReaders left out.
- [ ] New or refurbished confirmed; used devices left out.
- [ ] Resales, direct out-of-state shipments and reservation sales documented and excluded; federal sales kept in.
- [ ] Devices banded by viewable screen size (15 and 35 inches go up a band).
- [ ] Fee shown separately on receipts, or a statement on the receipt that the retailer paid it for the customer.
- [ ] Self-consumed devices reported.
- [ ] Lessor election to pay a registered vendor documented where used.
- [ ] 3% kept only by the collecting retailer or vendor; remainder paid.
- [ ] Return filed and paid by the last day of the month after the period.
