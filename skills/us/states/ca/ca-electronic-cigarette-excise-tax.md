# California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax (CECET), 12.5% at retail v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from the Healthy Outcomes and Prevention Education (HOPE) Act, Revenue and Taxation Code (R&TC) 31000 to 31008, read in the official California Legislature bulk data archive and the CDTFA Business Taxes Law Guide; Fee Collection Procedures Law sections 55042, 55044 and 55050 and R&TC 30101.7 in the same Law Guide; CDTFA's California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax page, its online filing instructions for the CECET return, and its Tax Guide for Cigarette and Tobacco Products Retailers (Getting Started and Industry Topics); and CDTFA's special taxes and fees rate table. All were read on 2026-11-23 (California, Pacific time). No accountant has reviewed it.

**Answer first.** "Beginning July 1, 2022, a purchaser shall pay a tax on the purchase for use in this state of an electronic cigarette from a retailer at the rate of 12.5 percent of the sales price of the electronic cigarette" (R&TC 31002(a)(1)(A)). The retailer collects it at the sale (R&TC 31002(a)(2)), shows it on the receipt as the "California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax" (R&TC 31003), builds it into any price shown on a sign or display (R&TC 31002(b)), keeps 1% as reimbursement and pays the rest with a **quarterly** electronic return due "on or before the last day of the month following each calendar quarter" (R&TC 31002(c)(3), (d)). Tax collected and not paid over is a debt the retailer owes the state (R&TC 31002(e)). CECET is charged "in addition to the sales and use tax", and sales tax is not charged on the CECET ([CDTFA CECET page](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/electronic-cigarette-excise-tax.htm)).

**What it covers.** Nicotine liquids sold on their own, and devices, parts and accessories "sold in combination with" a nicotine liquid (R&TC 31001(c)(1)). CDTFA reads "in combination" as items "packaged together as one unit or sold for a single price". Out: a device or delivery system "sold individually or with a liquid or substance containing 0mg nicotine", and FDA-approved cessation or therapeutic products sold only for that use (R&TC 31001(c)(2); CDTFA CECET page).

| Quarter | Return and payment due (R&TC 31002(d)) | Day of week |

| July to September 2026 | 31 October 2026 | Saturday |

No CDTFA rule moving a CECET due date that falls on a weekend was read; pay and file by the statutory date, or confirm with CDTFA.

**Tax year.** Tax year label 2026-27: this skill covers the quarters of the second half of 2026 and the first half of 2027. CDTFA's rate table shows 12.5% from 1 July 2022 to the present ([CDTFA special taxes and fees rates](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/special-taxes-and-fees-tax-rates/)). The archive's section history shows R&TC 31000 to 31004 and 31008 unchanged since SB 395 and 31005 last amended by AB 186 (2022); the CDTFA Law Guide histories match. Of the 1,263 bills chaptered in 2026 in the same archive, none names R&TC 31000 to 31008 in its title; the two 2026 vape bills (AB 762, disposable devices, and AB 2667, vape waste and advertising) amend the Business and Professions, Health and Safety and Public Resources Codes, not the tax.

## Scope and who this is for

In scope: retailers of electronic cigarettes containing or sold with nicotine, in California stores, vending machines and online, including out-of-state delivery sellers shipping to California consumers, and their bookkeepers and advisers. "Retailer" has the sales tax meaning (R&TC 6015) and includes vending machine operators and persons making sales regulated by Business and Professions Code 22963 (R&TC 31001(g)). The job: decide which sales carry CECET, compute it, show it correctly to customers, and register, file and pay on time.

Out of scope, and where it goes:

| Topic | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Cigarette and tobacco products tax on distributors (tobacco products 51.08% of wholesale cost from 1 July 2026 per CDTFA's rate table) and whether a product bears it | not covered here; CDTFA |
| Cigarette and tobacco products retailer license (CRL), distributor license and PACT Act registration | CDTFA licensing; one-line notes below |
| Flavored tobacco ban, Unflavored Tobacco List, disguised and disposable vape bans | CDTFA Industry Topics; one-line notes below |
| Sales and use tax rates and returns beyond how they interact with CECET | california-sales-tax; CDTFA |
| Covered battery-embedded waste fee on products with embedded batteries | the separate covered battery-embedded waste fee skill; not restated here |
| Cannabis vapes | cannabis excise tax; not covered here |

## Ask the client first

- Where does the business sell: California stores, vending machines, online, or shipping from outside California? How many retail locations hold a CRL?
- Does it hold a seller's permit, a CRL and a CECET permit (account)? If it ships from outside California, does it hold a distributor license, a tobacco products distributor tax account and a PACT Act account?
- For the quarter, what were sales of nicotine liquids, of devices and parts packaged or priced together with nicotine liquid, and of 0 mg liquids and devices sold on their own?
- Were handling charges billed, and did any order mix nicotine and non-nicotine items?
- Are shelf and online prices shown with the CECET included?
- Were any products FDA-approved cessation products, sold only for that use?
- Did any product sold count as a disposable, battery-embedded vape device (sale banned in California from 1 January 2028)?

### Steps

1. **Is it an "electronic cigarette"?** Under R&TC 31001(c)(1), any of: "(A) A device or delivery system sold in combination with any liquid substance containing nicotine that can be used to deliver to a person nicotine in aerosolized or vaporized form"; "(B) A component, part, or accessory of a device described in subparagraph (A) that is used during the operation of the device if sold in combination with a liquid substance containing nicotine"; "(C) A liquid or substance containing nicotine, whether sold separately or sold in combination with any device". CDTFA's examples: eCigarettes, eCigars, ePipes, vape pens and eHookahs, and "atomizers, vaping tanks, mods, batteries, or chargers", where "packaged together as one unit or sold for a single price" with nicotine; and "eLiquids or eJuices containing nicotine" ([CDTFA CECET page](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/electronic-cigarette-excise-tax.htm)). Not included: a device "sold individually or with a liquid or substance containing 0mg nicotine", and FDA-approved cessation or therapeutic products "if that product is marketed and sold solely for that approved use" (R&TC 31001(c)(2)). A 0 mg product still needs a CRL to be sold ([CDTFA retailer Getting Started](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/cigarette-and-tobacco-guides/retailer/getting-started.htm)).
2. **Is it a purchase for use in California?** "There is a rebuttable presumption that an electronic cigarette is purchased for use in this state" if it is bought from a retailer physically located in California or shipped to a California address (R&TC 31002(a)(1)(B)). A retail sale is as defined in R&TC 6007 (R&TC 31001(h)).
3. **Register.** A CECET permit (account) is required "Regardless of where a cigarette and tobacco products retailer is located". In-state: seller's permit, CECET account and CRL. Out-of-state delivery seller: CECET account, distributor license, tobacco products distributor tax account and PACT Act account, but no CRL; it may also need a seller's permit or certificate of registration for use tax. Several CRL locations share "only one CECET account" and report on one return ([CDTFA CECET page](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/electronic-cigarette-excise-tax.htm); R&TC 31002(f)). CDTFA treats a retailer outside California selling to California consumers as a delivery seller under R&TC 30101.7. That section allows delivery sales of cigarettes or tobacco products only if the seller meets every condition in 30101.7(d): Jenkins Act compliance, the licenses it would need if selling in California, state escrow and payment obligations on manufacturers, state and local restrictions on retail sales, and any Attorney General reporting ([R&TC 30101.7](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol3/ctptl/ctptl-30101-7.html)). Whether a given vape product is a "tobacco product" for that section turns on R&TC 30121, not read here.
4. **Price and receipt.** "A retailer shall include the amount of the tax imposed by this part in any price marketing on any sign or display inside or outside a licensed brick and mortar establishment" (R&TC 31002(b)). For each retail sale, the retailer gives a receipt or other document that "sets out and separately identifies the 'California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax' and the amount paid by the purchaser" (R&TC 31003).
5. **Measure the sales price.** "Sales price" has the sales tax meaning (R&TC 6011, not read here; R&TC 31001(i)). From CDTFA's CECET page:
   - **Handling is in.** "Handling charges are considered part of the sales price of electronic cigarettes for the purposes of the CECET". If one handling charge covers a taxable item and a non-taxable 0 mg device, "the handling charge should be prorated". CDTFA's example states as a fact that USD 5.00 of a USD 10.00 charge applies to each item; it gives no method for prorating.
   - **Mixed orders.** Only the nicotine item, or the device packaged or priced together with nicotine, is in. In CDTFA's USD 20.00 0 mg device plus USD 15.00 nicotine eLiquid example, the CECET shown is USD 1.88, which is 12.5% of the USD 15.00 eLiquid (rounded); the page's label "($20.00 × 0.125)" does not match that figure.
   - **CECET is out of the sales tax measure.** "Sales and use tax only applies to the total selling price of the electronic cigarette product, excluding the CECET."
6. **Compute.** CECET = 12.5% x taxable sales price. The return enters "the total retail sales of electronic cigarettes containing or sold with nicotine that are subject to the CECET", without sales tax, for all locations; the system applies the 12.5% rate, then the 1% "Less retailer reimbursement", giving the "Total amount of CECET after retailer reimbursement" ([CDTFA CECET return instructions](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/online-filing-instructions/eCigarette-Tax-Return.htm)).
7. **Over-collection.** "[A]ny amount unreturned to the purchaser which is not tax but was collected from the purchaser under the representation by the retailer that it was tax constitutes debts owed by the retailer to the state" (R&TC 31002(e)). If CECET was charged on something that is not taxable (a 0 mg device, say) and not refunded to the customer, report it in the return's "Excess CECET Collected" field: "If you have collected more tax than what is due (excess tax) for this reporting period, enter the amount of excess excise tax collected in this field" (CDTFA CECET return instructions).
8. **File and pay.** File the CECET return through CDTFA Online Services by the due date in the table above, with payment; "You must file all applicable cigarette and tobacco products tax returns, reports, and schedules even if you made no transactions or deliveries during the reporting period" (CDTFA CECET page). File the sales and use tax return separately. EFT is required at an average of USD 20,000 a month (R&TC 55050).
9. **Late.** "[A] penalty of 10 percent of the amount of the fee, together with interest on that fee at the modified adjusted rate per month, or fraction thereof" for late payment, 10% for a late return, and the penalties "limited to a maximum of 10 percent of the fee for which the return is required for any one return" (R&TC 55042(a) to (c)). For CECET, "fee" in the Fee Collection Procedures Law includes this tax (R&TC 31002(c)(2)). Relief for reasonable cause is available under R&TC 55044.
10. **Permit at risk.** CDTFA may, after 10 days' written notice and a hearing, revoke or suspend a CECET permit if the person fails to comply with the HOPE Act or a CDTFA rule or regulation under it, if its seller's permit has been suspended or revoked, or if its license under Business and Professions Code Division 8.6 (the CRL or distributor license) has been suspended or revoked (R&TC 31004(a)). "A person whose permit held pursuant to this part has been previously suspended or revoked shall pay the department a fee of one hundred dollars ($100) for the issuance of a permit" (R&TC 31004(d)). CDTFA's page lists, for reinstatement, clearing the cause, compliance, an active seller's permit and CRL or distributor license, and the USD 100 fee.

**AUDIT FLASH POINT** (what is "sold in combination" and the measure). A device, battery, charger or tank carries CECET only if sold with nicotine liquid, which CDTFA reads as "packaged together as one unit or sold for a single price". Stores that ring a device kit and a nicotine pod as separate lines at one visit, sell "0 mg" devices next to nicotine pods, or drop handling charges from the base are where the base can be wrong in either direction. Keep item-level records showing nicotine content and how each item was packaged and priced, and how handling was prorated. No HOPE Act regulation is listed in the CDTFA Law Guide (the HOPE entry lists the law only), so these lines rest on the statute and CDTFA's guidance pages.

## Boundary and exception table

| Situation | Result | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nicotine eLiquid sold on its own | CECET on its sales price | R&TC 31001(c)(1)(C) |
| Vape kit (device and nicotine pods) in one package at one price | CECET on the whole price | R&TC 31001(c)(1)(A), (B); CDTFA CECET page |
| Device sold on its own, or with 0 mg liquid | No CECET; CRL still needed to sell it | R&TC 31001(c)(1); CDTFA CECET page; CDTFA retailer Getting Started |
| USD 20.00 0 mg device and USD 15.00 nicotine eLiquid on one receipt | CECET on USD 15.00 only: USD 1.88 as shown by CDTFA | CDTFA CECET page |
| Handling charge covering a nicotine item and a 0 mg device | Prorate; CECET on the nicotine item's share | CDTFA CECET page |
| FDA-approved cessation product sold only for that use | Not an electronic cigarette | R&TC 31001(c)(2) |
| Order shipped to a California address by an out-of-state seller | Presumed for use in California; the seller needs a CECET account and must collect | R&TC 31002(a)(1)(B); CDTFA CECET page |
| Five CRL stores, one business | One CECET account and one return for all | CDTFA CECET page |
| Quarter with no sales | Return still due | CDTFA CECET page; CDTFA CECET return instructions |
| CECET charged on a 0 mg device and not refunded | Debt to the state; report as "Excess CECET Collected" | R&TC 31002(e); CDTFA CECET return instructions |
| Flavored nicotine eLiquid | Retail sale banned in California; seizure and fines; tax treatment of banned sales not covered here | CDTFA retailer Industry Topics |
| Disposable, battery-embedded vape device | Import or manufacture for sale banned from 1 January 2027; sale banned from 1 January 2028 | CDTFA retailer Industry Topics (AB 762, 2026) |

## Worked cases

Both cases are made up for illustration.

**Case 1: a vape retailer's January to March 2027 return.**

- **Facts:** a California business with two CRL stores and a website shipping only to California addresses; one CECET account. All products are lawful to sell in California, the website sales meet the conditions for delivery sales in R&TC 30101.7(d), and no CECET was over-collected. Sales for the quarter, before CECET and sales tax:
  - Nicotine eLiquids and pods sold on their own: USD 64,000.
  - Starter kits (device and nicotine pods in one package at one price): USD 18,000.
  - Devices sold on their own, with no liquid: USD 9,000.
  - 0 mg eLiquids: USD 3,000.
  - Handling charges on website orders: USD 800, of which USD 600 is the share prorated to nicotine items and kits (the retailer's records show the allocation).
- **Taxable sales price:** 64,000 + 18,000 + 600 = USD 82,600. The devices sold alone, the 0 mg liquids and USD 200 of handling are out.
- **CECET:** 12.5% x 82,600 = **USD 10,325.00**. Less retailer reimbursement 1% x 10,325.00 = USD 103.25. **Total amount of CECET after retailer reimbursement, payable with the return: USD 10,221.75**, due Friday 30 April 2027.
- **Sales tax, for contrast:** the CECET is not in the sales tax measure. The sales tax return measures the items sold (and any handling that is taxable under the sales tax rules) without the USD 10,325.00.

```oa-check
name: CECET quarterly return, January to March 2027, vape retailer
inputs:
  nicotine_liquids: 64000
  nicotine_kits: 18000
  devices_alone: 9000
  zero_mg_liquids: 3000
  handling_total: 800
  handling_nicotine_share: 600
  rate: 0.125
  reimbursement_rate: 0.01
steps:
  - taxable_sales_price: nicotine_liquids + nicotine_kits + handling_nicotine_share
  - excluded: devices_alone + zero_mg_liquids + (handling_total - handling_nicotine_share)
  - cecet: round(taxable_sales_price * rate, 2)
  - reimbursement: round(cecet * reimbursement_rate, 2)
  - payable: cecet - reimbursement
expected:
  taxable_sales_price: 82600
  excluded: 12200
  cecet: 10325.00
  reimbursement: 103.25
  payable: 10221.75
```

**Case 2: one customer, a shelf sign and an online order.**

- **In store:** a pack of nicotine pods priced at USD 16.00 before tax. The shelf sign must include the CECET: 16.00 x 1.125 = **USD 18.00** (R&TC 31002(b)). The receipt shows USD 2.00 as "California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax" (R&TC 31003). Sales tax is computed on USD 16.00, not USD 18.00.
- **Online:** the same customer orders a nicotine eLiquid at USD 40.00 and a 0 mg device at USD 24.00, shipped to a California address, with an USD 8.00 handling charge, of which the retailer's terms state USD 4.00 applies to each item (the same kind of stated allocation as in CDTFA's example; no proration method is taken from CDTFA). CECET = 12.5% x (40.00 + 4.00) = **USD 5.50**. Using the 8% demonstration rate from CDTFA's examples and treating the handling as in the sales tax measure (as CDTFA's example does), sales tax = 8% x (40.00 + 24.00 + 8.00) = USD 5.76. Order total = 72.00 + 5.50 + 5.76 = **USD 83.26**.

```oa-check
name: CECET shelf price and a mixed online order with prorated handling
inputs:
  pod_price: 16.00
  eliquid_price: 40.00
  zero_mg_device_price: 24.00
  handling: 8.00
  items_sharing_handling: 2
  rate: 0.125
  demo_sales_tax_rate: 0.08
steps:
  - shelf_price_with_cecet: round(pod_price * (1 + rate), 2)
  - pod_cecet: shelf_price_with_cecet - pod_price
  - handling_per_item: handling / items_sharing_handling
  - online_cecet_base: eliquid_price + handling_per_item
  - online_cecet: round(online_cecet_base * rate, 2)
  - online_sales_tax: round((eliquid_price + zero_mg_device_price + handling) * demo_sales_tax_rate, 2)
  - online_total: eliquid_price + zero_mg_device_price + handling + online_cecet + online_sales_tax
expected:
  shelf_price_with_cecet: 18.00
  pod_cecet: 2.00
  handling_per_item: 4.00
  online_cecet_base: 44.00
  online_cecet: 5.50
  online_sales_tax: 5.76
  online_total: 83.26
```

## When to refuse or refer

- **Is a product legal to sell?** Flavor ban, Unflavored Tobacco List, disguised and disposable vape bans, age rules: refer to CDTFA's retailer guidance, CDPH and the Attorney General.
- **Distributor-level tobacco products tax and licensing.** Not covered here; CDTFA.
- **Sales tax computation and local rates.** Use california-sales-tax and CDTFA.
- **Penalty relief, refunds, determinations and appeals.** Refer to CDTFA under the Fee Collection Procedures Law.
- **Cannabis vapes, nicotine pouches and other non-vapor products.** Outside the CECET definition as read here; refer.

## Filing and payment

- **Registration:** CECET permit (account) through CDTFA Online Services, together with the seller's permit and CRL (or, for delivery sellers, the distributor, tobacco products tax and PACT Act accounts) (R&TC 31002(f); CDTFA CECET page).
- **Return:** California Electronic Cigarette Excise Tax Return, quarterly, filed electronically (R&TC 31002(d)(2)), one return for all CRL locations. Lines: total retail sales subject to CECET (no sales tax), CECET at 12.5%, less retailer reimbursement at 1%, CECET after reimbursement, excess CECET collected, total CECET due (CDTFA CECET return instructions). CDTFA's separate filing video was not watched.
- **Due:** on or before the last day of the month after the quarter; CDTFA's own example is the quarter ending March 2026 "due on or before April 30, 2026" (CDTFA CECET page). See the table near the top for the 2026 and 2027 dates.
- **Payment:** through CDTFA Online Services by EFT or card; EFT mandatory at an average of USD 20,000 a month (R&TC 55050; CDTFA CECET page).
- **Records:** item-level sales showing nicotine content, packaging and price; handling allocations; receipts showing the CECET line. CDTFA's return instructions: "A copy of the return should be retained on the licensed premises for verification by CDTFA auditors". CDTFA's retailer guide: "Keep complete and legible purchase invoices for cigarettes and tobacco products for four years, and the latest 12 months of purchase invoices at the licensed location" (CDTFA retailer Getting Started).

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Seller's permit, CRL (or delivery seller accounts) and one CECET account in place.
- [ ] Each sale classified: nicotine liquid, device or part sold in combination with nicotine, 0 mg or device alone, FDA-approved cessation product.
- [ ] Shipments to California addresses and in-store sales treated as presumed California use.
- [ ] Handling charges included, and prorated on mixed orders.
- [ ] Shelf and display prices include CECET; receipts show the CECET line.
- [ ] CECET kept out of the sales tax measure.
- [ ] CECET at 12.5%, 1% reimbursement, return filed and paid by the due date, even with no sales.
