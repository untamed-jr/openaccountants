# California Tire Fee, USD 1.75 per new tire v0.1

> **General reference only.** This skill is general tax/accounting reference material for AI-assisted workflows. It has not been reviewed for any specific person's facts, documents, elections, deadlines, residency, filing status, or local procedures. Do not rely on it to file, pay, amend, or take a tax position without review by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from Public Resources Code (PRC) 42885 (both current versions: the one in force until 1 January 2034 and the one operative from that date) and 42886, read in the official California Legislature bulk data archive and the CDTFA Business Taxes Law Guide; CDTFA's Tax Guide for California Tire Fee (Getting Started and Industry Topics pages); CDTFA Publication 91 (3-23); and CDTFA's special taxes and fees rate table. All were read on 2026-11-23 (California, Pacific time). No accountant has reviewed it.

**Answer first.** "A person who purchases a new tire ... shall pay a California tire fee of one dollar and seventy-five cents ($1.75) per tire" (PRC 42885(b)(1)). The retail seller charges it as a separate charge, states it separately on the invoice, collects it at the sale, may retain one and a half percent of the fee "as reimbursement for any costs associated with the collection of the fee", and remits the rest quarterly (PRC 42885(b)(2), (b)(3), (d)). "The fees remitted pursuant to Section 42885 are due and payable quarterly on or before the 15th day of the month following each calendar quarter" (PRC 42886). CDTFA assigns "either a quarterly or annual reporting period" and a return is required even with nothing to report ([CDTFA Publication 91](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/pub91.pdf)).

| Quarter | Return and payment due |
| --- | --- |
| July to September 2026 | 15 October 2026 (Thursday) |

**From 1 January 2034** the current section is repealed by its own terms (PRC 42885(i)) and the later version takes over: USD 0.75 per tire, with the seller keeping 3% (PRC 42885, operative 1 January 2034).

**Tax year.** Tax year label 2026-27. The fee has been USD 1.75 since 1 January 2005 ([CDTFA special taxes and fees rates](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/special-taxes-and-fees-tax-rates/); [CDTFA Getting Started](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/california-tire-fee/getting-started.htm)). Both versions of PRC 42885 were last amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 42 (SB 175), effective 29 June 2024, per the archive's section history. No change for 2027 was found as of the read date.

## Scope and who this is for

In scope: tire dealers, auto repair shops, vehicle, trailer, motorcycle, e-bike, construction and farm equipment dealers, marketplace facilitators, lessors of vehicles and equipment, out-of-state sellers, and purchasers who did not pay the fee; and their bookkeepers. The job: decide which tires carry the fee, compute and collect it, and file and pay.

Out of scope, and where it goes:

| Topic | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Sales and use tax on tires and vehicles | california-sales-tax |
| Waste tire hauler registration, manifests, facility permits (CalRecycle) | refer |
| Disposal fees a shop charges for old tires | not the state fee; must be shown separately from it (PRC 42885(d)) |
| Civil and administrative penalties for false statements (PRC 42885(e), (f)) | refer |

## Ask the client first

- What does the business sell: replacement tires, vehicles or equipment with tires, or both? Does it hold a California Tire Fee account, and is it on a quarterly or annual period?
- How many new tires were sold at retail in the period, including tires and spares on vehicles, trailers and equipment sold? Any retreaded or used tires?
- Which tires went for resale, to buyers outside California by direct shipment, or to Native Americans on reservations? Which were for wheelchairs or disability vehicles?
- What kinds of vehicles or equipment: standard forklifts, golf carts, riding mowers and go-karts carry the fee only on separately sold replacement tires?
- Were any tires replaced under warranty or recall, at no charge or for a charge? Who paid for recall replacements?
- Does the business lease or rent vehicles or equipment on which it bought new tires without paying the fee?

### Steps

1. **Is it a new tire?** (PRC 42803.5, the "motor vehicle" definition it cites, was not read; trailers, e-bikes and similar items in this skill come from CDTFA's guidance.) "'New tire' means a pneumatic or solid tire intended for use with onroad or off-road motor vehicles, motorized equipment, construction equipment, or farm equipment that is sold separately from the motorized equipment, or a new tire sold with a new or used motor vehicle, as defined in Section 42803.5, including the spare tire, construction equipment, or farm equipment. 'New tire' does not include retreaded, reused, or recycled tires" (PRC 42885(g)).
2. **Exclusions.** The fee "shall not be imposed on a tire sold with, or sold separately for use on" a self-propelled wheelchair, a motorized tricycle or quadricycle (Vehicle Code 407), or a similar vehicle designed for a person who because of physical disability cannot otherwise move about as a pedestrian (PRC 42885(h)).
3. **Check CDTFA's product chart** ([CDTFA Industry Topics](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/california-tire-fee/industry-topics.htm)). Subject both as a replacement tire and when sold with the product: ATVs, backhoes, bulldozers, electric bicycles, electrically motorized boards, motorcycles, tractors, trailers, loaders, construction or agricultural forklifts. Subject only as a replacement tire sold separately: standard forklifts, go-karts, golf carts, riding lawnmowers, riding snow blowers, motorized skateboards. Not subject: aircraft tires, push mowers with motor, self-propelled snow blowers, rototillers (unless trailered). "Unlike traditional non-electric bicycles, electric bicycles are considered motor vehicles for purposes of the tire fee".
4. **Is the sale subject to it?** The fee does not apply to "Sales for resale", "Sales to Native American occurring on Indian reservations", or new tires "that the retailer ships directly to a location outside California when the transaction is not subject to California sales or use tax"; "Sales to the United States Government are not exempt from the California Tire Fee" (CDTFA Industry Topics).
5. **Charge, show and collect.** Charge it as a separate charge and state it separately on the invoice; "Any other disposal or transaction fee charged by the retail seller related to the tire purchase shall be identified separately from the California tire fee" (PRC 42885(b)(2), (d)). Keep it out of the sales tax measure: "Do not include the fee amount in your sales and use tax calculation" (Publication 91).
6. **Special cases** (CDTFA Industry Topics):
   - **Leased or rented vehicles and equipment:** a lessor that buys a vehicle or equipment with new tires without paying the fee reports and pays it; on a lease buyout, the fee is collected on "any new tires you mount on the vehicle or equipment at the time of the sale", and not if no new tires, or only used tires, are mounted.
   - **Defective tires:** "If the customer is not charged for new tires that are replacing defective tires, then the customer does not owe the tire fee on the replacement tires"; if the customer pays anything, the customer owes the fee and the retailer collects it. CDTFA's defective-tire paragraph does not say whether anyone else owes the fee on a free replacement.
   - **Manufacturer recalls:** where the manufacturer pays, reimburses or credits the retailer for free replacements, "the manufacturer owes the tire fee on the replacement tires" and "the retailer of the tire must collect the tire fee from the manufacturer".
   - **Warranty replacements:** if the customer pays anything, collect the fee; "If the retailer does not charge the customer for the replacement tires, then the retailer must pay the tire fee owed directly to us."
   - **Out-of-state sellers:** register and collect if required to hold a California seller's permit or use tax registration; otherwise the California purchaser pays CDTFA directly.
7. **Compute the remittance.** Fee = new tires subject to the fee x USD 1.75; the seller may keep 1.5% and remits the rest (PRC 42885(b)(3)).
8. **File and pay** the CDTFA-501-TF online by the 15th day of the month after the period, every period (PRC 42886; CDTFA Getting Started; Publication 91).

**AUDIT FLASH POINT** (counting tires on vehicles and equipment). The fee follows the tire count, including the spare on every new or used vehicle sold, and turns on whether the product is a motor vehicle, trailer, construction or farm equipment (fee on tires sold with it) or only "motorized equipment" (fee only on separately sold replacement tires). E-bikes, electrically motorized boards, ATVs and trailers are frequently missed; standard forklifts, golf carts and riding mowers are frequently over-charged when sold with tires. No-charge warranty replacements, where the retailer must pay the fee itself, are another common gap.

## Worked cases

Both cases are made up for illustration.

**Case 1: a tire shop's October to December 2026 quarter.**

- **Facts:** a tire shop on a quarterly period sold 2,400 new tires in the quarter. Of these, 160 went to another dealer on a resale certificate, 40 were shipped directly to customers in Oregon in transactions not subject to California sales or use tax, and 40 were for self-propelled wheelchairs. It also sold 30 retreaded tires.
- **Tires subject to the fee:** 2,400 - 160 - 40 - 40 = 2,160. The retreads are not new tires.
- **Fee collected:** 2,160 x 1.75 = USD 3,780.00. **Kept (1.5%):** USD 56.70. **Remitted:** **USD 3,723.30**, due 15 January 2027.

```oa-check
name: California tire fee, tire shop, October to December 2026
inputs:
  new_tires_sold: 2400
  resale: 160
  shipped_out_of_state: 40
  wheelchair_tires: 40
  fee_per_tire: 1.75
  retention_rate: 0.015
steps:
  - tires_subject: new_tires_sold - resale - shipped_out_of_state - wheelchair_tires
  - fee_collected: round(tires_subject * fee_per_tire, 2)
  - retained: round(fee_collected * retention_rate, 2)
  - remitted: fee_collected - retained
expected:
  tires_subject: 2160
  fee_collected: 3780.00
  retained: 56.70
  remitted: 3723.30
```

**Case 2: a powersports and vehicle dealer, and what changes in 2034.**

- **Facts:** in January to March 2027 a dealer sells 30 new cars (four tires and a spare each), 10 motorcycles, 15 electric bicycles, and 8 standard forklifts with their tires. It also sells 40 replacement forklift tires separately.
- **Tires subject:** cars 30 x 5 = 150; motorcycles 10 x 2 = 20; e-bikes 15 x 2 = 30; forklifts sold with tires 0 (standard forklifts carry the fee only on replacement tires sold separately); replacement forklift tires 40. Total 240.
- **2027:** 240 x 1.75 = USD 420.00; kept 1.5% = USD 6.30; remitted USD 413.70, due 15 April 2027.
- **Same sales after 1 January 2034:** 240 x 0.75 = USD 180.00; kept 3% = USD 5.40; remitted USD 174.60.

## When to refuse or refer

- **Product classification** beyond CDTFA's chart: contact CDTFA.
- **Waste tire management** (haulers, manifests, storage): CalRecycle.
- **Penalty relief, refunds and appeals:** CDTFA under the Fee Collection Procedures Law (PRC 42885(c)).
- **Sales and use tax:** california-sales-tax.

## Filing and payment

- **Registration:** CDTFA online services, "Register a New Business Activity", for a California Tire Fee account. Required for California retailers of new tires (including on vehicles and equipment), marketplace facilitators, lessors of vehicles or equipment with new tires, and purchasers who did not pay the fee, including retailers that take new tires from inventory for their own use (CDTFA Getting Started).
- **Return:** CDTFA-501-TF, California Tire Fee Return, online, quarterly or annual as assigned; required even with no activity (CDTFA Getting Started; Publication 91).
- **Due:** "on or before the 15th day of the month following each reporting period" (CDTFA Getting Started); see the table near the top.
- **Payment:** online; EFT if CDTFA requires it, initiated by the due date (CDTFA Getting Started).
- **Late:** "The penalty for filing a late return and/or late fee payment is ten percent of the fee amount due for the period", plus interest (CDTFA Getting Started).

## Completion checklist

- [ ] New tires counted, including tires and spares on every vehicle, trailer and qualifying equipment sold.
- [ ] Retreaded, reused and recycled tires left out; wheelchair and disability-vehicle tires left out.
- [ ] CDTFA's product chart applied (forklifts, golf carts, riding mowers: replacement tires only).
- [ ] Resales, direct out-of-state shipments and reservation sales excluded; federal sales kept in.
- [ ] Fee shown separately on invoices; disposal fees shown separately from it.
- [ ] Warranty, recall and lease situations handled.
- [ ] 1.5% kept; remainder paid with the CDTFA-501-TF by the 15th of the month after the period.
